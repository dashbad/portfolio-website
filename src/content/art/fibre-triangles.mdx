summary: |-
  Its quite common in Singapore for drivers to decorate their vehicles with "leaky" fibre optic cables. They give a similar effect to COB LED strips, with a softer more uniform glow. 

  I was ideating on a large piece to place above my DJ Booth and came upon this idea. It was the first time I had fully designed and built an LED fixture from scratch using Fusion 360 and my 3D Printer. 

  I chose to mount it on white mdf, however if I were to repeat it I would mount on clear acrylic and use clear wires to hide the interconnections. It would also be interesting to make the design more modular in nature

  The Controller is conceaed in one of the central nodes
