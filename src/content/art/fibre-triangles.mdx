## Overview

*Placeholder copy — replace with the project narrative.*

Fibre Triangles is a free-standing lattice of thirty-six triangles, each edge a bundle of
side-glow fibre coupled to its own RGBW illuminator. Because each triangle is individually
addressable the piece can render slow geometric animations across the whole structure.

## Concept

A study in **structure as pixel**: the lighting resolution is the frame itself. Patterns are
generated from the adjacency graph of the triangles rather than a pixel grid.

## Electronics & control

The driver HAT provides 36 constant-current RGBW channels over SPI. Patterns are authored
in TouchDesigner and streamed as sACN, with a standalone Python fallback that runs on boot.
