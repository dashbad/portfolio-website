## Overview

*Placeholder copy — replace with the project narrative.*

Geodesic Dome is a five-metre walk-in structure whose every strut is a diffused LED bar.
Ultrasonic sensors at each hub track people moving through the space and send ripples of
light out across the shell.

## Concept

An inhabitable circuit diagram. The dome's own geometry — struts as edges, hubs as nodes —
becomes the topology for the light simulation, so animations propagate along real physical
paths.

## Electronics & control

| Node   | Struts | LEDs  | Sensors |
| ------ | ------ | ----- | ------- |
| North  | 22     | 1,540 | 4       |
| South  | 22     | 1,540 | 4       |
| Crown  | 21     | 1,470 | 2       |

Nodes keep a shared clock over ESP-NOW so that patterns spanning multiple nodes stay in
phase. Strut sleeves are opal polycarbonate tube, which gives an even glow without hotspots.
