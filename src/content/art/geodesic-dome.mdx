summary: |-
  I've always been fascinated by Geodesic domes since studying them at University and learning about Buckminster Fuller - a very inspiring individual. 

  This began as an exercise in teaching myself how to model a geodesic dome before adapting it into a light fixture. 

  It consists of 160 LEDs embedded in a dual layer dome with 1mm transparent diffusers controlled by WLED
