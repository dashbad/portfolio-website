summary: |-
  This was my first LED project. Full credit for the original model goes to Paragami (paragami.com), which I modified to make the pieces hollow and designed the backboard and frame.

  The frame is constructed from mahogany with mitres and rabbets cut with hand held power tools. Despite this, the hardest part was achieving a nice painted finish on the frame. I also allowed too much tolerance between the pieces so there are small visible gaps up close under natural light
