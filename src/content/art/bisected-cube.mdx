summary: |-
  I wanted to experiment further with the PMMA fibre used in Fibre Triangles but in 3 Dimensional space.

  The cube is made of two halves, joined at the lower rear corner

  3D printed plastic is not the best material for this application as there is some flex in the structure, requiring me to print a small spacer to keep the closest edges spaced equally apart
