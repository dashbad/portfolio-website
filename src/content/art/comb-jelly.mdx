summary: |-
  This began as an expirment in modeling using the Form tool in Fusion 360, to see how easy it is to create non-geometric shapes. 

  The inspiration is a  comb Jelly. 

  The construction is based around an inner form that the LEDs are mounted to, contained within a transparent outer shell.

  I am not completely happy with the outcome. Mounting the shells to the base was very tricky and I had hoped the LEDs would diffuse better through the outer shell. My original plan was to use the same technique as I used for the fibre triangles however this did not produce a bright enough light.
