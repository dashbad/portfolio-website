## Overview

*Placeholder copy — replace with the project narrative.*

Comb Jelly is a suspended light form that breathes. Eight rows of LEDs run the length of a
segmented spine, each row driven by a travelling-wave pattern so that light appears to ripple
down the body like the cilia of a ctenophore. Layered polycarbonate skins diffuse the pixels
into soft bands of colour.

## Concept

The piece explores **bioluminescence as a slow signal**: motion that is perceived rather than
seen, and colour that shifts over minutes rather than seconds. Wave speed and hue are
modulated by ambient sound level in the room.

## Electronics & control

| Signal        | Pin      | Notes                              |
| ------------- | -------- | ---------------------------------- |
| LED data 0–3  | GPIO 4–7 | 74HCT245 level shifted             |
| LED data 4–7  | GPIO 8–11| 74HCT245 level shifted             |
| Mic (I²S)     | GPIO 12–14 | INMP441, 16 kHz                  |
| Art-Net       | Wi-Fi    | Universe 0–15, fallback to internal |
